Why isn't it on Google Play?

Our apps are free, open source and collect nothing. Publishing on Google Play means registering with Google, handing over government ID and paying a fee. Google is now extending that identity check beyond its own store: since 30 September 2026, apps from participating app stores in Brazil, Indonesia, Singapore and Thailand must come from developers verified by Google, and Google plans to require this for all apps on certified Android phones worldwide from 2027.

We believe you should be able to install software you choose on a phone you own, without asking Google's permission, so we support Keep Android Open and distribute directly instead. If your phone one day refuses an app from an unverified developer, Android's advanced flow (in Developer options; a one-time 24-hour wait) or an install from a computer still works.
